🌟 Lucent GK Studio: Master Guide & Rulebook

1. Retention Status & Mastery (The 3-State Cycle)

Every question tracks familiarity using an interactive color-coded Seen Circle:

  • 0 / Unseen (Dark Slate): Brand new or never reviewed questions.
  • 1 / Seen Once (Gray): Reviewed or attempted once.
  • 2 / Seen Twice (Orange): Reinforced through secondary review.
  • ✓ / Mastered (Green): Automatically locked when answered correctly across 2 separate spaced sessions.

2. Spaced Repetition & Leitner Mistake Recovery

  • Spaced Repetition: Correct answers dynamically push your next review interval further out (1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 days).
  • Mistake Bank Recovery Queue: Missed questions enter a strict 5-stage Leitner spaced recovery pipeline (24h → 48h → 7d → 30d → 90d). You must answer a missed question correctly 3 times to clear it.

3. Study Workspaces & Modes

  • Practice Mode: Instant feedback, detailed solutions, personal mnemonic notes, and flexible navigation.
  • Exam Simulation Mode: Enforces a countdown or stopwatch timer with bulk submittal at the end.
  • Card Inspection: View saved or mistaken cards with full resumable text-to-speech audio controls (Play / Pause / Resume / Stop).
  • Mind Map Atlas: Visual repository for flowcharts and diagrams with zoom viewport and arrow-key navigation.

4. How to Master Things (Actionable Strategy)

  1. Clear your Due Cards daily from the dashboard queue.
  2. Hammer down recurrent errors using the Mistake Bank recovery queue.
  3. Bookmark high-yield facts using the ⭐ Star Question button.
  4. Pair MCQs with the Mind Map Atlas to build spatial memory.
